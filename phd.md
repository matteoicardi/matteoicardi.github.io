# PhD opportunities

I am looking for PhD students who enjoy applied mathematics, from analysis to modelling to coding. The projects below are listed on the [School of Mathematical Sciences PhD projects page](https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/mathematics/study/research/postgraduate-projects.aspx), which also explains funding and how to apply. If one of them interests you, email me a short note and your CV.

- **Does a peat bog remember its own age?** Creep, decay and what a peat core can and cannot tell us. ODEs, PDEs, mechanics.
- **Why do peat bogs make stripes and mazes?** Pattern formation in a thin, soft layer. Linear stability, thin films, simulation.
- **What happens to chocolate in your mouth?** Breaking, melting and dissolving crystals, and reduced models that stay physically possible.
- **Scientific computing for porous materials: a multiscale solver in C++ and OpenFOAM.** For people who like programming as much as maths.
- **Well-posedness and stability of physics-informed data fitting.** Analysis of fitting data and a PDE at the same time.
- **When does a flowing fluid become a wall?** Clogging, constraints and Lagrange multipliers, with OpenFOAM simulations.
- **Charging batteries faster: ions, electric fields and data.** Reduced models for porous electrodes, tested against detailed simulations and data.

Other ideas are welcome, especially at the boundary between mathematics, computation and real materials.
