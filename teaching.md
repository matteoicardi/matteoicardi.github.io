# Teaching

I like teaching mathematics as a tool for understanding real things: how a structure moves, how heat spreads, how a fluid flows. I enjoy most the courses where students set up a model themselves, solve it by hand or on a computer, and then check whether the answer makes sense.

### What I teach

- **First-year mathematics for scientists and engineers:** calculus, linear algebra, vectors and complex numbers, for Mathematics, Natural Sciences and Engineering students.
- **Applied mathematics and mechanics:** statics, dynamics, and differential equations with applications.
- **Differential equations:** ODEs, phase plane and stability, an introduction to PDEs (heat and wave equations, method of characteristics), and analytical methods such as asymptotic expansions and power series for engineers.
- **Scientific computing:** numerical methods for ODEs and PDEs, with applications to advection, diffusion and transport (MSc and PhD level).
- **Multiscale modelling:** random walks, volume averaging and homogenisation, in PhD courses and summer schools.
- **Computational fluid dynamics:** hands-on courses with open-source tools such as OpenFOAM.

I have taught at Nottingham, Warwick and Politecnico di Torino, and in short courses at KAUST, UT Austin and the CISM summer school in Udine. I am an Associate Fellow of the Higher Education Academy.
