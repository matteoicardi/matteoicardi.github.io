I am an applied mathematician working on flow and transport in complex materials: soils, rocks, filters, batteries, and peatlands. I build mathematical models across scales, analyse them, and turn them into open-source simulation tools, mostly in OpenFOAM. I lead the **[MultiForm](https://github.com/MultiForm-UoN)** group (Multiscale Fluid Dynamics and Porous Media) and coordinate the **[MATHPEAT network](https://mathpeatnet.work/)** on the mathematics of peatlands and soft porous materials.

### Research in short

- **Peatlands and soft porous materials:** how bogs grow, creep and record their own history. [More](peat.html)
- **Multiscale modelling and homogenisation:** replacing complicated microstructures by simpler averaged equations, and knowing when that works.
- **Data and models together:** stability of physics-informed and data-driven models, inverse problems, uncertainty quantification.
- **Computation:** finite-volume methods and open-source solvers for porous media, electrokinetics, clogging and multiphase flow. [Software](coding.html)

### Background

Before joining Nottingham in 2017 I was a Zeeman Lecturer at the University of Warwick, and a postdoc at UT Austin (ICES), KAUST and Politecnico di Torino, where I did my PhD in Chemical Engineering and my degrees in Mathematical Engineering.
