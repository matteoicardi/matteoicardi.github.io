# Research

I combine mathematical modelling, analysis and open-source computation to understand flow and transport in complex materials. Full publication list: [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=KbKLuDYAAAAJ&hl=en).

### Themes

- **Multiscale modelling and homogenisation:** from pore-scale physics to averaged equations, and when the averaging fails.
- **Peatlands and soft porous materials:** mechanics, growth and patterns of bogs. [More](peat.html)
- **Data-driven and physics-informed models:** stability of combining data and PDEs, inverse problems, uncertainty quantification.
- **Transport in porous media:** dispersion, mixing, reactions, deposition and clogging.
- **Electrochemistry and batteries:** ion transport in porous electrodes and reduced models.
- **Particulate and food systems:** population balances, crystallisation, freezing, and the oral processing of chocolate.
